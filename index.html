Mayank Sanganeria

I'm an engineer and computer musician. I build music software, and I make music, art, and the occasional research paper. I studied at CCRMA, Stanford, and I'm based in San Francisco. Say hello if you'd like to collaborate.

Music tools

Free, browser-based tools for musicians — built to make practising and studying music a little easier.

Real Ear Trainer

Train your ear — recognise intervals, chords, and progressions by sound.

realeartrainer.com

Real Sight Reader

Sight-reading practice and drills built from your own repertoire.

realsightreader.com

Music Ed

Hear the musical examples from classic theory and orchestration textbooks, beside the score.

textbook.realmusictheory.com

Whozart

A daily listening quiz — hear a piece, name it, and share your score.

whozart.com
